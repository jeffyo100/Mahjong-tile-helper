TilePal

Your mahjong rack's best friend

Put TilePal on your iPhone in a few taps

Once it's on your home screen it opens like any other app. No download from the App Store.

The TilePal link
https://tilepalapp.com
  1. 1

    Open the link in Safari

    Use the blue compass app called Safari. If you tapped the link inside a text or another app, choose Open in Safari first.

  2. 2

    Tap the three dots

    Look for the three dots in the bottom right corner of the screen.

    More
  3. 3

    Tap "Share"

    It's in the menu that pops up. It has a square with an arrow pointing up.

    Share
  4. 4

    Tap "View More," then scroll down

    Keep scrolling the list until you find Add to Home Screen. It has a little plus in a square.

    Add to Home Screen
  5. 5

    Tap "Add"

    That's it. The TilePal icon is now on your home screen. Tap it any time to play.

Want the line code?

The line code is the list of hands TilePal checks your rack against. Tap the button, send the email that opens, and it will be sent back to you.

Request the line code

Button not opening your email? Write to tilepalapp@gmail.com and ask for the TilePal line code.

How to upload the line code

You'll get the line code as a link. To load it into the TilePal on your home screen:

  1. Press and hold the link, then tap Copy.
  2. Open TilePal from your home screen and tap Settings, the gear in the top corner .
  3. Under Backup & share, tap Clear field, paste, then tap Load cards.

If something looks different

  • No "Add to Home Screen" in the list? You're probably not in Safari. Open the link in Safari and try again.
  • On some iPhones the Share button sits right at the bottom of the screen, a square with an arrow pointing up. If you see it there, tap it and skip the three dots.
  • On an Android phone, open the link in Chrome, tap the three dots, then Add to Home screen.
  • Your hands and settings are saved on the phone you add it to, so each phone needs its own card loaded.
